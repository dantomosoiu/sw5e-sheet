# SW5E Character Sheet Viewer (Chrome extension)

One scrollable page with everything about your SW5E character, plus a sticky summary/sidebar to jump between sections. Works on phones and tablets too.

## Install (unpacked)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, switch on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the `sw5e-sheet` folder.
4. Pin the extension (puzzle icon → pin).

## Your first session
1. **Get your character.** Load from SW5E brings in your sw5e.com characters; New character… (More ▾) builds one here.
2. **Roll.** Click any number: a skill, a save, Initiative. The dice open at the bottom: press Roll (or Enter). Shift-click rolls at once.
3. **Attack.** Weapons & Attacks: click the to-hit number, roll, then Hit: roll damage (or Critical damage).
4. **Take damage or heal.** Combat & Vitals: type the amount, then Damage, Heal or Temp HP. At 0 HP the death saves appear there.
5. **Use a feature.** Class Features: Start (Rage, a stance, a form) or Use; it's listed under Effects until you End it. Uses come back on rests.
6. **Cast a power.** Force or Tech Casting: Cast on the power's card spends the points; its damage or healing rolls from the card.
7. **Use an item.** Equipment & Inventory: Use on a medpac, stim or grenade, on you or on someone else.
8. **Rest.** Combat & Vitals: Short rest (spend Hit Dice there to heal) or Long rest.

Everything you change is kept in this browser; **Sync to SW5E** sends it to sw5e.com, and **More ▾ → Back up this character** saves a file. The same steps are in the sheet: **More ▾ → First session guide**.

## Use
- On a sw5e.com character page, click the extension icon: the sheet opens in a new tab and loads that character.
- Anywhere else, click the icon and press **Load from SW5E** (uses your sw5e.com session; opens sw5e.com briefly in the background if no tab is open).
- **Import file…** accepts SW5E's character export JSON or a snapshot exported from this viewer.
- **New character…** (More ▾, or the first page) walks through name and species (with its ability increases), class (skills, tools), ability scores (standard array, point buy, or manual/rolled, with species and feat bonuses shown), background (skills, tools, languages, feat and its +1), starting equipment (each "(a) or (b)" choice resolved to SW5E's items; packs and story items become custom items) or starting wealth, and personality (pick or roll from the background's tables). The review lists what's still open. The character is kept here; **Save to sw5e.com** creates it on your account.
- **Level up** (Classes & Progression, or the XP line when you have the XP): choose the class, or a new class (its multiclass requirements are checked), hit points (the average or a roll), the Ability Score Improvement (+2, +1 and +1, or a feat with its text) and the archetype when its level comes; it shows what the level brings. New powers, maneuvers and options are then picked in the class card, whose counts update. Overview lists everything **still to choose** (class and background skills, tools, languages, a feat's +1, an empty ability score improvement, the archetype, options, maneuvers and powers below their counts), each linking to where it's chosen.
- **Combat & Vitals** tracks play state (HP, dice, points, death saves, rests): damage at 0 HP counts as a failed death save (massive damage as three: the HP card then says Dead, with a "GM ruling: at 0 HP instead" link), dropping to 0 HP ends concentration and Rage, and every damage, heal or counter change says what it did and can be undone, one step at a time (the last ten). Rests say what came back and end the effects that would have run out (a short rest: an hour or less; a long rest: 8 hours or less, and concentration); a long rest removes one level of exhaustion (two with Frenzy). After a short rest the message offers **Roll a hit die** when you're hurt and features that give points back (**Use Force Recovery (+6 force points)**). Healing features (Second Wind) roll their healing from their counter and spend the use with **Heal**. States (Rage, forms, Force Shield…) can be started and ended from the tracker as well as from their cards. **Conditions** (Poisoned, Prone, Stunned…) are set in the tracker: the dice roller applies what they do (disadvantage, advantage, automatic failures), Speed shows their effect, and the phone strip shows them; dropping to 0 HP makes you Unconscious. Damage can have a **type**: resistances and immunities the sheet knows apply ("Took 4 damage (8 energy, halved (Rage))"). Damage while **concentrating** offers the Constitution save (War Caster's advantage included) and ending the power if it fails; at 0 HP, Second Heart and the like are offered. **Combat → Start** counts rounds: Next round ends effects that have run out. **Your own effect** (Effects) adds a temporary bonus or reminder the sheet doesn't know. Exhaustion says what each level does, and the dice roller applies its disadvantage. **Edit** on Overview, Equipment, Personality, Backstory and Notes changes XP, credits, alignment, personality, appearance, backstory and notes. Equipment & Inventory is always editable: quantities, equip/unequip, attune, remove, a credits box (+n / −n or a new amount), and adding items from SW5E's own item list (fetched from sw5e.com on first use and kept in this browser). Added items are free unless you tick Pay; the price defaults to the standard price and can be changed. Consumables and items with charges or uses (e.g. an Antitoxkit's five charges) have a **Use** button: one charge at a time, and the item goes when its last charge or last unit is used. Items with an effect ask whether you use them **on yourself** (the effect starts) or **on someone else** (just used up); a healing item says how many HP you're missing and can't be used on yourself at full HP. Quantity, equip, attune and remove changes, ending an effect and removing a pick say what changed, with **Undo**. A medpac or repair kit used on yourself rolls its healing in the dice roller (dice the size of your Hit Die + your Constitution modifier, at least 1; no Hit Die is spent) with a **Heal** button; a vitapac gives spent Hit Dice back.
- **Inventory:** the item search matches every word in any order ("fragmentation grenade"), shows prices and SW5E's rarities with their usual names ("Premium (uncommon)"), and filters enhanced items by type and minimum rarity. **Equipment packs** (Explorer's, Diplomat's…, read from SW5E's Equipment chapter) are added as what's in them; a pack kept as a custom item has **Unpack**, and starting equipment unpacks them too. Anything not in SW5E's list can be added as a **custom item** (weight, cost, description). Any item can get **your own name and a note** (✎). An **enhanced weapon or armor** is tied to its base item (one you carry, or one added with it). It shows as one row with the base's damage or AC and weight; Weapons & Attacks lists it under its own name with the bonus; equipping or removing it does both. Paying has a **discount %** and **from** whom. When you're short, **Pay what you have, owe the rest** or **Owe it all**; **Debts** list who you owe, with Pay back and Settled. Rows show what you paid. **Sell** removes items and adds credits (half the standard price by default). Credits keep a **history** with a note per change. Weapons with **reload** count their shots (each attack rolled here uses one); **Reload** takes a power cell or cartridge from your inventory. Bulky or Obtrusive armor gives disadvantage on Stealth in the roller, and an unmet Strength requirement or Cumbersome armor lowers your speed.
- **Class choices** (Classes & Progression): archetype, maneuvers (with how many you can know and why, e.g. table + Malleable Maneuvering), and each class's option list (Discoveries, Exploits, Instincts, Strategies, Auras, Vows, Routines, Ideals, Force-Empowered Castings), with prerequisites and archetype additions. Custom features that were workarounds for these can be converted in one click. Features SW5E's data leaves out are filled in from the class text; every feature has a notes box, and a feature whose choice is made in a picker (Fighting Style, the archetype feature, Berserker Instincts) says what was chosen there. With the keyboard, the "Add…" and "Learn…" lists add what you land on only when you press Enter (a click or tap adds it at once); the archetype can be chosen from its level (3rd). **Read the options** under each list shows every option with its text and a Choose button. The class card also picks the class's skills (the multiclass skill and tool for a second class), Expertise and tools; the Background section its skills, tools, languages and the feat's +1; Species its extra languages; Feats the +1 of a feat taken at an ability score improvement. A skill already chosen elsewhere isn't offered again.
- **Class features at work:** limited-use features get counters (read from their text: Rage, Second Wind, Channel the Force, Focus…) that reset on rests; class-table dice (Sneak Attack, Martial Arts, Potent Aptitude…) are rollable. Features change the numbers like effects do: ability increases, unarmored AC and speed, proficiencies the engine misses (e.g. "martial vibroweapons with the finesse property"), and the choices a feature asks for. Those get pickers on the feature's card, with how many to choose: "one of the following" options (Force Affinity's Ashla, Bendu or Bogan; Bendu then adds your other modifier to force points), which ability gets an increase, a skill "Persuasion or Intimidation", tools of a kind ("one musical instrument of your choice"), weapons ("two vibroweapons or blasters of your choice"), damage types. The picks are kept in the feature's notes, next to anything you write there. Expertise is picked in the class card, skills and tools together. Bonuses that grow with level, "a bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier", wider critical ranges (shown on the attack roll before you roll), half proficiency on checks (Led by the Force) and unarmed die steps apply too. Features you switch on (Rage, Critical Analysis, stances and forms, Force Shield…) have a **Start** button: it spends a use, applies the state's numbers (e.g. Rage Damage on melee Strength weapons, plus what other features add while raging), advantages (pre-selected in the roller) and defenses, and lists the state's benefits under Effects until you End it.
- **Fighting styles, forms and other picks:** whatever a feature, class option, feat or species lets you choose from SW5E's other lists gets a picker with its count, in the class card (or under Feats / Species): fighting styles and masteries (Fighter, Guardian, Scout, Fighter's Instinct, Fighting Stylist…), lightsaber forms (a Form archetype's own form, Formfighting Dabbler's one/three/seven), weapon focuses and supremacies (tick the weapons they cover: SW5E's data doesn't group weapons), class/multiclass/splashclass improvements, extra maneuvers and superiority dice (Superiority Style), and powers given by feats, features and species (Force-Sensitive, Tech Dabbler, "You learn the *burst* force power"). Picks that give more picks nest (Formfighting Style → three forms). Problems are shown where they are: too many, prerequisites ("At least 3 levels in…", "the ability to cast force powers", another option or maneuver), a style, form or power chosen twice, a feat taken twice. Counts like "if you already know at least one lightsaber form" look at what you had before. Picks kept for a feat or level the character no longer has are listed under **Stored choices not in use**. Their numbers apply like class features (focus +1 damage on the chosen weapons, supremacy's wider critical range on those weapons only); forms are **Adopt**ed as a state until your next turn. Archetypes' own lists count too (Hunter's Prey, Elemental Adept, totems, ammunition enhancements). **Engineer modifications** are installed in the modified item you pick from your inventory, with slots from the engineer table (two items from 9th level where the archetype says so), their prerequisites, bonuses on that weapon, and the tech point penalty for each modification beyond your proficiency bonus.
- **Powers:** each casting class and archetype has a picker in its class card, counted by its table's Powers Known, up to its Max Power Level, with power prerequisites (the Mechanist's table replaces the Scout's). Force/Tech Casting lists every power you know, also those given by feats, features and species (marked with where they come from; powers a feature only lets you cast are marked "cast only"), and casts them: pick the level (level + 1 points), with each class's once-per-long-rest levels (Consular 6th–9th, Guardian 4th–5th, archetype casters 4th…; in a multiclass the class with the most levels decides, force and tech apart). Once-per-rest, free and feature-use casts are tracked (Telekinetic Ministrations, System Override, Techie's free cast), the Vow of the Devoted spends focus points, a concentration power ends the one before (not when cast on yourself where a feature says so), a power that lasts a while is listed under Effects until you End it, and you can't cast while raging unless Furious Force allows it. DCs and attacks use the casting ability a feature names (Evil Eye, species, the Archaeologist's Intelligence). With casting from two classes, the multiclass Max Power Level table applies.
- **Powers and class resources in play:** a power's damage, healing or damage reduction is rollable on its card ("Damage 1d6 force", "Healing 1d8+5"), at the level you cast it (Force Potency, Overcharge Tech) and with at-will damage growing at 5th, 11th and 17th level. After **Cast**, the message offers the roll and, for healing, **Heal N (on me)**. A power that helps you (Battle Precognition's AC 13 + Dex, Saber Ward's resistance, Target Lock's extra 1d6 on weapon damage, Battle Meditation's d4) applies while it lasts. One that helps the creature you cast it on (Heroism, Guidance) asks **It's on me: apply it**. Heroism's temporary hit points are offered each round, and Guidance's d4 is ticked on your next check, then the power ends. **Force-Empowered Casting** options you know (Quickened, Twinned…) are chosen next to Cast and add their points. At-will powers that last a while or roll something (Saber Ward, Saber Reflect) have Cast too. Ashla's Force Affinity offers +level hit point maximum after a light side power. In the dice roller, attacks and damage offer what you can spend: maneuvers that add the superiority die, **Force-Empowered Strikes** (+1d8 per force point, up to the Focused Strikes column), **Close Call** with your modified weapon. Ticking one spends it (unticking gives it back). **Weapon Expert** offers damage advantage (the weapon's dice twice, the higher set counts), and Mercantile offers its expertise on business Persuasion checks. **Force Visions** rolls two foretelling d20s on each long rest (or on demand in the tracker); a d20 roll offers **Use N** before rolling. **Reckless Attack** and fighting-style **marks** (Sharpshooter, Defense Style) start from the tracker or their card. **Rage with Frenzy** is free the first time after a long rest, then adds a level of exhaustion when the rage ends. **The Way of the Mynock** applies Saber Ward and counts its Saber Reflect reactions (each one rolls the reduction). **Channel the Force** has a Use button per effect you have (the class one you chose, written to its notes, plus your form's), with its save DC and amount; Lend Aid offers Heal. **Infuse Item** picks the armor or weapon (+1, +2 at 10th, +3 at 15th) until your next long rest.
- **Species rules SW5E's engine leaves out** apply too:
  - A hide or carapace ("13 + your Dexterity modifier while unarmored or wearing light armor") is an AC formula. The best formula counts, and the AC hover lists the one not used.
  - Natural weapons (Zabrak, Wookiee Claws, Cathar) set the unarmed die. With finesse, the unarmed strike uses Dexterity when it's better.
  - Powerful Build and Brawny double carrying capacity, and together triple it.
  - Each ability score lists where its increases come from: the species, each Ability Score Improvement, the background feat's +1, tweaks.
  - A tweak that was a workaround for something the sheet now applies to the same number (its reason names the trait or feature) is flagged in the tweak list: it counts twice.
- **Rules at hand:** a weapon or armor property ("two-handed", "versatile (2d4)", "reload 16") explains itself on hover, focus or tap, from SW5E's rules text. Equipping a two-handed weapon and a shield together is flagged when you equip, on the weapon's row and on its attack roll. An AC "set to" tweak is shown next to AC, and equipping armor or a shield while it's there says the tweak overrides it.
- **Engineer modifications:** a modified weapon is marked "modified" in Weapons & Attacks, with its modifications and what they do; what a modification does when the weapon deals damage (Celerity Oscillator) is noted on the damage roll. A modified item that requires attunement counts toward attunement.
- **Lightsaber forms:** the form cards and the form pickers have the general form rules (adopted as a bonus action on your turn, one at a time, each chosen once). The Guardian archetypes named after forms are explained as class paths, separate from the forms you adopt.
- **Defenses & senses:** Combat & Vitals lists resistances, immunities, advantages on saves, senses and other speeds, gathered from class features, options, active states, species traits, feats and equipment (a jetpack's flying speed is marked "when switched on"); Vision shows the species' darkvision. Species traits that give a skill or tool proficiency apply it ("You have proficiency in the Survival skill"; for a choice such as Coercive's, write it in the trait's notes).
- **Tweaks** (Settings, Tweaks & Data) are manual adjustments, from sw5e.com's editor or added here (the list of what to tweak is grouped: Combat first, then ability scores, saves, skills, powers, maneuvers, weapons): a bonus, a "set to" value, proficiency/expertise for skills and saves, or a weapon's attack/damage. Give each a reason; tweaks without one are flagged. Like effects, every number a tweak changes is underlined and explains itself on hover.
- **Effects** (Combat & Vitals) lists everything that changes the character's numbers, read from SW5E's own item text: enhanced items that are equipped, installed, used as a focus or attuned (e.g. Shield (Fine) AC +1, a belt that sets Strength to 21, a weapon upgrade applied to the weapon you choose), and temporary effects from consumables used on the character or effects someone gave you (**Add an effect**). Ability scores, AC, speed, HP maximum, saves, skills, initiative, tech/force attack and save DC, weapon and unarmed attack/damage are applied by SW5E's rules engine as temporary tweaks; conditional bonuses are shown as alternative values next to the stat they affect, wherever it appears: AC "13 · 14 vs melee · 12 vs ranged", tech attack "+5 · +6 melee tech attacks", save DCs for powers needing particular saves, a weapon's attack and damage "with Cryo Cell (Average)" when you carry ammunition it fires, saves "vs poison". Other traits (resistance, advantage…) are listed as notes. Every number an effect changed is underlined: hover (or focus) it to see the value without effects and each effect behind it, with how long temporary ones last. Effects are never saved to sw5e.com; temporary ones stay until you press **End**.
- **Dice:** click any number on the sheet (or anywhere on a skill or save row) to roll it: ability checks, saving throws, skills, initiative, tool checks (Proficiencies), weapon attacks and damage, tech/force attacks, maneuver attacks, the superiority die, hit dice and death saves; or type a roll (e.g. `2d6+3`) in the dice dock (**🎲 Dice**). Before rolling, choose advantage/disadvantage, conditional bonuses ("vs melee", "with Cryo Cell"), extra dice and a modifier (Enter rolls; Shift-click rolls straight away). After rolling, all of these can still be changed: dice already rolled keep their numbers and only added dice are rolled. A hit die, death save or superiority die goes on the tracker in one click: **Heal N and spend the d8**, **Record a success** (two failures on a natural 1, 1 hit point on a natural 20), **Spend a superiority die**. A weapon attack offers its damage roll right away (critical damage is suggested on a natural 20; a natural 1 says Miss; a versatile weapon also offers its two-handed damage; Sneak Attack is dimmed after an attack rolled with disadvantage). **★ Use Inspiration** gives a roll advantage and spends your Inspiration (shown with a ★ in the phone strip). The breakdown names every part ("d20 17 (advantage) + 1d4 2 + 3 modifier"). A **DC** box shows Success or Failure. The dock can be made small (▾) so the sheet stays clickable, and while it's open it never shrinks under the pointer. Critical damage is **Double dice** (roll the damage dice twice) or **Max + roll** (one set counts at its maximum, plus one set rolled); the choice is remembered until changed, and switching it on a roll already made keeps the first set's numbers. Recent rolls (the last 40 of the character on screen, shared by every open viewer tab) are kept and can be reopened and amended. Escape closes the dock; results are announced to screen readers. A damage roll's breakdown names each part of the bonus: "1d8 6 + 3 DEX modifier + 2 Brute Force + 1 Xerrol Nightstinger Rifle". A character's latest 15 rolls go with it when you sync, and come back when it's loaded on another computer.
- **On a phone or tablet** the section list becomes a strip pinned to the top. It has **☰** (every section, plus Load from SW5E, Import, New character, Recent messages, backup, compact cards and theme), your HP and AC, and what's active (Rage, a form, concentration…; tap it to get to Effects, where it ends). Then come the sections, a 🔍 filter and the 🎲 dice. The strip doesn't slide sideways while you're using it, and the highlighted section is the one you're reading. Tables turn into lists (the class table too), and messages show as short notices under the strip. **Compact cards** (More ▾; on by default on phones) shows only each card's title, counters and buttons (uses, Start, Cast, save DC); tap a card to read it.
- Press `/` to filter: cards, rows and boxes without the text are hidden, cards that have it open, and empty sections drop out (Esc clears). Enter or Space opens and closes the focused card. Cards and sections you opened or closed stay that way when the sheet updates, and the page doesn't jump: what you just used stays where it was. **More ▾** has export, recompute, expand/collapse all, compact cards and theme options; **Remove from this viewer** asks once more, since changes not synced to SW5E go with it.
- **Steady clicks:** section jumps are instant. If the sheet redraws and a different button ends up under the pointer (the next row's × after removing one, + instead of Use), a click there in the next moment is ignored, and the sheet says so (Undo stays). A click on the same button goes through. Notices stay 6 s (10 s with a button such as Undo); **Recent messages** (More ▾, or ☰ on phones) lists the last 15.
- When sw5e.com can't be reached to recalculate the numbers, the sheet says so (with **Try again**) and retries a few times, then waits until you change something.

## Your data: kept here; the rules are SW5E's
- **This viewer's copy is your character's data**: the character as loaded from sw5e.com plus everything changed here (fields, inventory, play state, tweaks and their reasons, effects, custom values). It's kept in the extension's storage, with a version history.
- **The numbers are computed here, with SW5E's rules**: the sheet has its own rules engine, which works the numbers out the way sw5e.com's does (quirks included), from SW5E's rules and item data kept in this browser. Changes, effects and tweaks show at once and need no connection. Until that data is here (it's fetched from sw5e.com the first time it's needed), sw5e.com computes. **Checked against sw5e.com:** each Load from SW5E brings sw5e.com's own calculation, and the sheet compares it with its own; **Settings, Tweaks & Data → Rules engine** says whether they agree (or lists the numbers that differ, with **Copy the details** for a report), and **More ▾ → Check the numbers with sw5e.com** asks for a fresh one. Creating a character and levelling up happen here (written in the fields its engine reads, so sw5e.com shows the same after a sync); its builder isn't needed. **Load from SW5E** joins the site's changes with yours: what only the site changed is taken (a level added there, say what it brings), lists take both sides' additions, and where both changed the same thing yours wins (the message names it). **One at a time…** in the banner takes sw5e.com's value for a single field. Load switches to the character open on sw5e.com, or to one that's new there.
- **Sync to SW5E** is optional: it updates the copy on sw5e.com with your data (not with reasons, effects or other things its format can't hold), and a character made here is saved as a new one. After a sync, the sheet lists what sw5e.com will show differently (effects and features its engine doesn't apply, e.g. "Armor (Fine): AC +1"). If the site's copy changed since it was loaded, nothing is synced: the newer copy is merged in for you to check. If sw5e.com doesn't answer (an expired sign-in, usually) it stops after a while and says to sign in again there.
- **Not a backup:** sw5e.com's character storage is in beta and can lose characters. **More ▾ → Back up this character** saves one character to a file (its data and your changes, play state, tweak reasons, effects, version history and recent rolls); **Back up all characters** does the same for every character in this browser. Import either file to restore it. Back up regularly and keep the files somewhere safe: removing the extension deletes what it keeps. **Export for sw5e.com** is the character in SW5E's own format (with your changes, without play state, reasons or history); **Export SW5E's copy with its numbers** is sw5e.com's copy and its computed numbers, without your changes (for checking numbers or reporting a problem).
- **Version history** (Settings, Tweaks & Data): every version of the character seen here is kept. **Compare** shows what differs from the character now (classes and levels, items added or removed, credits, notes…); **Restore** makes it that version again as local changes (play state stays; Undo puts it back; Sync to SW5E sends it). The card says when this character and all characters were last backed up.

## How it works
The sheet's rules engine (`engine.js`) computes a character from SW5E's character data and the rules and item lists the extension keeps (fetched once from sw5e.com). It is built to give exactly what SW5E's own engine gives, and checked against it: on hundreds of made-up characters and the test characters (`dev/engine-check.js`), and on every character loaded from sw5e.com. The extension still reads characters from sw5e.com, and asks sw5e.com's engine (`character/generateCompleteCharacter`, in a sw5e.com tab) for its calculation on Load and on **Check the numbers with sw5e.com**.
Loaded characters are cached locally (chrome.storage) so the sheet works offline.

Syncing goes through the site's own `character/saveCharacter` action, and only when you press **Sync to SW5E**. It starts from the latest copy on sw5e.com and changes only play state and the fields above (inventory lists and tweaks are sent whole). If the character changed on sw5e.com since it was loaded here, nothing is sent: the newer copy is taken in, your changes are kept, and you check them before syncing again. The version being replaced is kept in the local history first.
